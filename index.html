Orthodontic Treatments for Children, Teens, and Adults

Orthodontic treatment is not limited to a particular age group. Children, teenagers, and adults can all benefit from orthodontic evaluations and appropriate treatment.

Traditional Metal Braces

Metal braces are a widely recognised orthodontic solution for correcting crooked teeth, overcrowding, gaps, and bite irregularities. They use brackets and wires to gradually move teeth into their desired positions.

Traditional braces can address a wide range of orthodontic concerns and are suitable for many patients who need comprehensive tooth alignment.

Clear Braces

Clear braces offer an alternative for patients who prefer a less noticeable orthodontic option. They work similarly to traditional braces but use tooth-coloured or transparent brackets to create a more discreet appearance.

They can be a suitable choice for teenagers and adults who want to improve their smiles while maintaining a more natural-looking appearance during treatment.

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable orthodontic appliances designed to gradually straighten teeth using a series of customised trays. They are popular among adults and teenagers who prefer a more flexible and discreet approach to orthodontic treatment.

Patients can remove their aligners when eating, drinking certain beverages, brushing, and flossing. However, successful treatment depends on following the orthodontist's instructions and wearing the aligners as recommended.

Common Dental Problems Orthodontic Treatment Can Correct

Many people seek orthodontic treatment because they experience dental alignment or bite-related concerns. An orthodontic evaluation helps identify the underlying issue and determine the most appropriate treatment.

  • Crowded Teeth: When there is insufficient space for teeth to fit properly within the jaw.
  • Gaps Between Teeth: Noticeable spaces between individual teeth that may affect smile appearance.
  • Overbite: When the upper front teeth extend significantly over the lower front teeth.
  • Underbite: When the lower front teeth sit in front of the upper front teeth.
  • Crossbite: When some upper teeth bite inside the lower teeth.
  • Open Bite: When the upper and lower front teeth do not meet when the mouth is closed.

Correcting these issues can improve dental function, bite alignment, and smile appearance.

When Should You Visit an Orthodontist?

Many parents wonder when their child should first visit an orthodontist. An initial orthodontic evaluation is generally recommended around age seven, as this allows an orthodontist to identify developing alignment or jaw concerns.

However, orthodontic treatment is not just for children. Adults can also benefit from braces and clear aligners. If you notice crowded teeth, difficulty cleaning between teeth, bite problems, or unwanted spaces, scheduling an orthodontic consultation can help you understand your options.

Early evaluation does not necessarily mean immediate treatment. It simply helps identify potential concerns and determine whether monitoring or intervention may be beneficial.

What to Expect During Your Orthodontic Consultation

Your first orthodontic consultation is an opportunity to discuss your dental concerns, smile goals, and available treatment options.

During the evaluation, the orthodontic team may:

  1. Examine your teeth, gums, and bite alignment.
  2. Review your dental and orthodontic history.
  3. Take photographs, digital scans, or X-rays when needed.
  4. Identify alignment or jaw-related concerns.
  5. Discuss suitable orthodontic treatment options.
  6. Explain the expected treatment process and follow-up appointments.

This personalised approach helps patients understand their options before beginning treatment.

Maintaining Your Smile During Orthodontic Treatment

Taking care of your teeth during orthodontic treatment is essential for achieving the best possible results. Braces and aligners require consistent cleaning and attention.

  • Brush your teeth at least twice daily using fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss regularly to remove food particles and plaque.
  • Follow your orthodontist's instructions for caring for braces or aligners.
  • Avoid hard and sticky foods if you wear traditional braces.
  • Clean removable aligners as instructed.
  • Attend scheduled orthodontic appointments.
  • Wear your retainer as recommended after treatment.

Maintaining good oral hygiene throughout treatment helps protect your teeth and gums while your smile improves.
